Mario Kart DS Controls and Things You Should Know:

W for gas, A/D to steer, SPACE to drift. Click "options" on title screen to change username. ENTER to select on the selection screens. SPACE to go back. SHIFT to take screenshot. E to use a powerup. (Currently red and blue shells only, and only you can use them!) Resolution slider sets resolution of textured tri filler, setting to 0 will make things further out less detailed, but closer things will have high detail. Wireframe further out will be filled in as you get closer, it was a way to show all of the scene and still have performance. Note that it was made to run in a laggy environment, so the framerate limit is 15 FPS. Don't be surprised if it is running below 20 FPS. NOTE: Supports controller/gamepad. Nintendo layout for description: A for gas, left stick to steer, R/ZR to drift. L/ZL to use powerup. Press Home on controller to restart project. Supports individual Joy-Cons.
